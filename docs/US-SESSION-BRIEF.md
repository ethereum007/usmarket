# Saved-session research brief

The US workspace starts each selected package with a concise brief, reusing imported feature and setup outputs. It does not introduce a new signal engine. Up to five candidates are sorted by existing 20-session benchmark-relative strength, with symbol tie breaks, 60-session evidence and exact detected-setup counts. Negative leaders still trail the benchmark. Missing/non-finite RS never becomes zero.

Ranking requires available features and resolved instrument-snapshot identity, and excludes known session gaps and instrument exclusions. Legacy packages without identity evidence remain viewable but cannot produce brief candidates. Available-feature coverage and rankable coverage are separately disclosed; custom membership does not represent the whole US market. The selected package date, final/partial state, benchmark and feed remain visible. Historical/partial research is never described as current market conditions. Partial packages retain the existing prohibition on saved plans.

Inspect actions clear queue filters and select the exact stock for existing charts, SEC evidence and conditional planning. Earnings date/timing, industry leadership and strategy edge remain explicitly unknown/unverified. This is the first daily-journey increment, not completed outcome review or a verified earnings calendar.

Tests cover ranking/counts, gaps/unresolved identities, unknown/non-finite values, bounded deterministic membership, partial/historical disclosures, table accessibility semantics, empty evidence and exact inspection actions. Real browser/mobile/keyboard journey acceptance, hosted accounts/storage and outcome review remain open in issue #10.
